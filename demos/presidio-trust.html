01 · ORG The Organization And Role's Core Purpose ›
The organization

Presidio Trust is a federal agency that stewards the Presidio — one of the most visited national park sites in the country, and also a place where people live and businesses operate — under a triple bottom line: loved and accessible to the public, a model of environmental stewardship, and excellent in its own operations.

03 · CLOSE Closing ›

A portfolio of 160+ systems — procurement, facilities, GIS, analytics, cloud, AI-enabled tools — isn't really 160 separate problems. It comes down to two things done well, continuously: knowing exactly who can touch what across every system, and making sure the workflows connecting those systems are actually doing what they're supposed to do. Most of the work isn't building something new. It's keeping what's already running configured correctly, connected correctly, and inside its access and compliance lines — then having the judgment to know when a vendor upgrade, a new integration, or a new AI tool is worth bringing into that portfolio versus adding risk to it.

That's the environment I've operated in for 13+ years. At Signant Health, I owned the data infrastructure behind 50+ concurrent clinical trial programs — a heavily regulated space where access control, audit integrity, and data accuracy weren't nice-to-haves, they were the job. Zero critical data-integrity failures across that entire tenure wasn't luck; it came from treating every system change, every integration, and every permission as something to get right the first time, because stakeholders were making real decisions on that data.

My approach to problem-solving is root-cause diagnosis: sit with the person (physically or virtually) actually affected, walk through their real workflow, and find the actual issue instead of the reported one. If it's an internal issue that isn't documented — no user to sit with, no ticket describing it — I investigate the same way: trace the workflow end to end, system by system and log by log, and diagnose where it's actually breaking down instead of guessing at a fix. That's the pattern behind a real-time monitoring and alerting system I built that caught errors before they became incidents, a query re-engineering pass that cut execution time 35%, and a database release pipeline I automated that cut backend workload 60% — in every case, fixing the root cause and documenting it so the fix holds up without me in the room.

An organization like Presidio Trust has to answer to the public, to environmental stewardship, and to its own operational integrity at the same time. That means being precise about who has access to what, and disciplined about making sure every system in the portfolio is actually doing its job. That's the work I've done for over a decade, and it's the work I want to keep doing.

I'm based in Daly City, right next to San Francisco and the Presidio — no relocation, no delay. This is posted as a remote role, and I've done that before: in-office at Signant Health until 2017, then fully remote from there until the layoff.

I'm a firm believer that the best way to know if someone will actually do a job well is to put them in the fire — a probation period — and see if they meet or exceed what's asked of them. I hope reading through this made it clear I'm capable, sharp, a quick learner, experienced, and educated enough to be worth that shot.
